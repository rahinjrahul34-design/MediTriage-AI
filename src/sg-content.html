MediTriage AI · Design System v1.0

Calm, trustworthy, Indian-friendly healthcare UI

Tokens, components, motion and copy guidelines powering the MediTriage prototype — WCAG AA, multilingual (EN·हिन्दी·मराठी), offline-aware. Inspired by the NHS Service Standard’s accessibility rigour and the clean blue-white language of leading Indian health platforms.

01 Color

Trust-building blues with a warm marigold accent. Small text on colour always uses the AA-tuned derivative (4.5:1+ against white); the raw brand hue is reserved for large text, icons and graphics (3:1+).

07 Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Built in

  • Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 text, ≥ 3:1 graphics (AA-tuned derivatives)
  • Full keyboard paths with visible 3-px focus ring + skip link
  • ARIA: labelled icon buttons, aria-pressed toggles, live-region toasts/chat, role=dialog modals with Esc close
  • 44–58 px touch targets; ≥ 14 px minimum text
  • lang="hi" / lang="mr" on Devanagari runs
  • Status never shown by colour alone; reduced-motion honoured

Voice-first inclusivity

  • Everything readable is also speakable (mic on every flow start)
  • Recognised speech is echoed back in writing for confirmation
  • Large-type mode scales tokens (user setting, 125%/150%)
  • Named emergency route (108) pinned on triage screens
