Designed for trust, tuned for India

From the first symptom to the final prescription, MediTriage keeps patients, doctors and records connected — even on a 3G village connection.

Voice-first AI triage

Speak naturally in Hindi, Marathi or English. The assistant confirms what it heard, symptom by symptom, before any conclusion.

Explainable results

Every urgency level shows its working: recognised symptoms, risk factors and plain-language reasoning — never a black box.

Truly multilingual

UI, voice and notices in multiple Indian languages with Devanagari-tuned typography, dd/mm/yyyy dates and larger touch targets.

Secure video consults

WebRTC calls with end-to-end encryption, automatic quality fallback, in-call chat and a waiting-room device check.

Records you own

Offline-first

Made for Bharat

The last mile is our first priority

Over 65% of India lives outside metros, where the nearest specialist can be a day away. MediTriage brings the first clinical opinion to the phone people already have.

  • Works on 3G & offline — cached records, queued triage, audio-first fallback.
  • Accessibility built-in — WCAG AA contrast, keyboard paths, screen-reader labels.
  • IoT vitals — BLE oximeters and BP monitors sync straight into the consult.
  • Aligned to ABDM — ABHA IDs and India-hosted, consent-driven data exchange.
